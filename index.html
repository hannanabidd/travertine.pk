Brand Introduction

Pakistan's
Signature Stone.

travertine.pk was founded on a single conviction: that the world's finest natural stone deserves to be delivered to global architecture with the precision and care it demands.

Our travertine originates from the geological formations around Loralai, Balochistan — a region known for producing stone of exceptional purity, consistent veining, and enduring structural integrity. We operate from quarry to fabrication to export under one roof, giving us complete control over the quality of every consignment we ship.

The result is stone that carries a single geological identity — ensuring harmony across your entire project, from the first block to the last tile.

Responsible Sourcing

Stone That
Respects the Earth.

Natural stone is among the most enduring materials in construction. At travertine.pk, we take that responsibility seriously — ensuring our quarrying and fabrication practices minimise waste at every stage.

Approach 01

Optimised Block Cutting

Every block is assessed and cut to maximise usable yield, reducing waste from the point of extraction.

Approach 02

Offcut Reuse

Material offcuts from slab production are retained and repurposed for our accessories and furniture range — nothing of value leaves the facility as waste.

Approach 03

Reduced Extraction Impact

By integrating block supply, slabs, and accessories under one production cycle, we reduce the need for additional quarrying to fulfil secondary orders.

Approach 04

Lower Carbon Footprint

Consolidated production and export logistics mean fewer shipments, smaller carbon exposure per project delivered.

"travertine.pk is committed to responsible quarrying and environmentally conscious craftsmanship — because the best stone should leave the earth better than it found it."

How We Work

From Enquiry to
Delivery — Made Simple.

01

Enquiry

Submit your specification — stone type, dimensions, finish, quantity, and destination port. We respond within one business day.

02

Sample Review

Receive physical samples of your selected stone. Approve the material before any production begins.

03

Order Confirmation

Agree pricing, lead time, and shipping terms. We issue a proforma invoice and commence processing upon payment confirmation.

04

Fabrication & Quality Check

Your order is cut, finished, and inspected in our facility. Pre-shipment inspection reports are available on request.

05

Shipment & Documentation

Your consignment is packed, containerised, and loaded. Full shipping documents are provided within 48 hours of vessel departure.

06

Ongoing Relationship

We remain your point of contact for re-orders, additional varieties, or new project specifications.
